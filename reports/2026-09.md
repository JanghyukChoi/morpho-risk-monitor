## Limits

- The panel is 11 days deep, so any streak reported as 11 days
  is censored — the market may have been impaired far longer. Streaks at the
  panel limit are marked with ≥.
- Nominal supply is the protocol's own figure and is inflated by interest accruing
  on positions that cannot be repaid. See CURATION.md for the reconstruction.
- A market appearing here is not a claim about any curator. It is a market I would
  not allocate to, published in advance.
